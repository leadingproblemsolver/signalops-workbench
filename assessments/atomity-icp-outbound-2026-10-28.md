3. ICP ENGINE: HARD GATES THEN SCORING
G0 — Is the company a genuine potential buyer or a provider/partner/competitor?
G1 — Does public evidence indicate relevant cloud, infrastructure, AI or data sovereignty complexity?
G2 — Is there a dated trigger, such as infrastructure expansion, migration, new AI deployments, governance change, or regulatory obligation? Industry membership alone is not a trigger.
G3 — Can we identify an accountable role: budget owner, operational champion and security/compliance evaluator? A known title is not a verified reachable individual.
G4 — Is there an appropriate Atomity entry product and testable first conversation? If not, RESEARCH rather than SEND.
Scoring of gate-passing candidates (100): dated trigger 30; depth of documented infrastructure fit 25; buyer identifiability 15; business consequence hypothesis 15; reachable channel 10; strategic reference value 5. Scores are internal prioritization, not measured conversion probability. Unconfirmed factors score zero, not inferred points.
States: WATCHLIST → SOURCE VERIFIED → PERSON VERIFIED → QUALIFIED → APPROVED TO CONTACT → SENT → REPLIED → DISCOVERY → PILOT → WON/LOST. BOUNCED and REJECTED are distinct. Never mark a sourced company a qualified buyer solely from its sector.
4. BUYER PERSONAS
Economic buyer — CTO/CIO/VP Infrastructure; accountable for strategic platform cost, operational risk and workload placement. In a cost-focused department, CFO/VP Finance may co-sponsor.
Champion — Head of Cloud/Platform Engineering, Infrastructure Director, FinOps Lead; owns day-to-day placement, spend and engineering tradeoffs.
Evaluator/blocker — Enterprise Architect, CISO, Cloud Security Lead, Data Protection/Compliance; validates integrations, audit records, policies, security and residency.
Partner channel — cloud MSPs, infrastructure consultancies and sovereign providers; may distribute rather than purchase Atomity. Keep them in a separate partner funnel.

9. EXPERIMENT, OUTCOMES, STOP RULES
Pilot: qualify 10 accounts; advance top 3 only if G0–G4 pass; identify 2–3 people per qualified account; send up to 3 approved messages, manually.
Measure: source-backed qualified accounts; verified people; delivered messages (exclude bounces); substantive replies; discovery bookings; current solution learned; pilot evaluation requests.
Hypotheses: (H1) infrastructure expansion yields more useful conversation than regulation-only targeting; (H2) technical champions engage before executive buyers; (H3) provider partnerships form a separate funnel with different economics.
Stop if no verifiable trigger, no rightful buyer, restricted contact, or no relevant product fit. After 3–5 well-qualified attempts, revise segment/message based on response evidence rather than scaling to hundreds.
No claim of sent outreach, booked calls, validated cost loss, meetings or actual pilot results is made in this assessment.
