SignalOps operator research

Give us one ugly logistics exception.

We are testing where real exception handling slows down — not assuming the software thesis is already correct.

1. Exception happens
2. Evidence is split
3. Who owns it?
4. Action happens
5. Is it truly resolved?

The painful step may be somewhere else. That is exactly what this research is trying to discover.

A 2–3 line example is enough.

Delayed shipment, failed delivery, warehouse discrepancy, port/customs issue, damaged cargo, incorrect status, missing document, or another consequential exception.

  • What happened?
  • What made it slow or uncertain?
  • What finally counted as resolved?
What we return:

A one-page reconstruction of the actual workflow: trigger → evidence → people/systems → ownership → action → verified settlement. The operator can correct it, reject it, or use it internally.

Evidence state: market hypothesis under direct operator evaluation. No adoption or impact claim is implied.
